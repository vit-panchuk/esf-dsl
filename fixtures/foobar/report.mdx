## The machine

Foobar has shipped every Tuesday for six years <Chip kind="observed" />.
Its build takes forty minutes <Chip kind="observed" />, which the team calls
fine <Chip kind="user" /> and the logs call optimistic <Chip kind="inferred" />.
Nobody has measured the queue <Chip kind="assumed" />.

<Finding deck="Ships weekly. Nobody knows why it works." thread="Foobar ships every Tuesday and nobody can say why it works." tag="observed">
Ships weekly. Nobody knows why it works.
</Finding>

<Takeaway>
A cadence nobody understands is a cadence nobody can defend when it breaks.
</Takeaway>

<Callout kind="unknown" label="What would change this">
One week of queue timings. If the median wait is under a minute, the forty
minutes is the build and not the machine around it.
</Callout>

<PullQuote>
If the build is red on a Tuesday, ship anyway.
</PullQuote>
